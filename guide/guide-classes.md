<h3 id="necromancer">Necromancer</h3><p><strong>Good for these jobs:</strong> Pet Class, General Workforce, Farmer (via undead), Construction (via undead), Cook (via the Lich evolved form only)</p><p><strong>Bonuses:</strong> -20°C min comfortable temperature, +40% Immunity gain speed, +40% Toxic resistance, +40% Global learning factor</p><p><strong>Penalties:</strong> -0.30 Move speed, -10% Mental break threshold, -50% Trade price improvement, -25% Negotiation ability</p><p><strong>Restriction:</strong> None on the Necromancer directly, but the undead it raises have real limits — see below.</p><p><strong>How undead work:</strong> Raised from dead pawns or animals. They don't eat, sleep, need recreation, or feel pain, and they heal rapidly and resist disease. They're tied to the Necromancer that raised them — separated from every available Necromancer (including by that Necromancer's death), an undead dies within a few in-game hours, unless someone's carrying a Necrotic Orb to stand in. Undead humanlikes can't do intellectual labor and have zero ranged skill, but they can fight in melee and do growing, construction, crafting, and dumb labor. Worth knowing: healing magic damages the undead, so a well-meaning healer in the colony is a real trap for them, not a benefit. Witnessing an undead being raised gives most nearby pawns a -8 mood hit.</p><p>You can't install implants in an undead pawn — surgery on them isn't possible.
