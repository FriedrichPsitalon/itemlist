### Priest

**Good for these jobs:** [Doctor](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#doctor) (dedicated), [Combat Support / Healing](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#combat-support)

**Bonuses:** +2 Medical skill, +0.15 Move speed, +20% Immunity gain speed, +25% Medical surgery success chance, +25% Medical tend quality, +25% Medical operation speed, +25% Medical tend speed

**Penalties:** -10% Pain shock threshold — Priests go down in a fight more easily than most

**Restriction:** Incapable of violence — can't hunt, can't fight, period

**Spells learned as part of the class:**
- **Advanced Heal** — a stronger, more levelable version of Paladin's Heal
- **Purify** — cures deep injuries and natural conditions, also reduces addiction withdrawal
- **Healing Circle** — AoE healing pulse
- **Bestow Might** — single-target combat buff

**Master spell (needs a scroll, not automatic):**
- **Resurrection** — revives a recently dead pawn, with less resurrection-sickness risk as the Priest levels up

**Also learnable via scroll, not native to the class:**
- **Heal** — the base version Paladin starts with
- **Flash Heal** — instant single-target full heal

Any medical-work implant — hands (Manipulation) and eyes (Sight).

</details>

<details open markdown="1">

**Armor**

- **Disciple Hood** (8) — +25% max energy, +5% regen, +15% arcane resist.

### Commander

**Good for these jobs:** [Morale](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#morale) (aura half), [Combat Support](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#combat-support) (commands half)

**Bonuses:** +40% Social impact, +30% Negotiation ability

**Penalties:** -20 Carrying capacity, -30% Global work speed, -40% Research speed — (genuinely bad at menial and research work)

**Restriction:** Social below 10 runs risk of commands failing (Social × 10%).

**Runs on Stamina, not mana** — same resource system as the other physical/fighter-type classes, not the mage classes.

**Auras (passive, toggled on — mostly useful outside combat):**
- **Provisioner Aura** (60 stamina to maintain) — improves rest and reduces hunger for nearby pawns. Upgrades to also repair equipment, boost natural healing, and boost energy regen.
- **Taskmaster Aura** (70 stamina) — boosts work speed, eating speed, and carrying capacity for nearby pawns, but makes them less social and gives them a mood penalty while it's active.
- **Commander Aura** (80 stamina) — improves mood for nearby pawns but lowers their productivity. Also has the Commander actively talk down pawns having a mental break; pawns saved this way don't get Catharsis afterward.

**Commands (active, used in combat):**
- **Stay Alert!** (80 stamina) — nearby pawns instantly gain rest and increased sight.
- **Move Out!** — nearby pawns get a burst of movement speed and can't be staggered or stunned, at the cost of reduced movement right after, while they recover.
- **Hold the Line!** — nearby pawns gain damage resistance, pain tolerance, mental stability, and increased fire rate, at the cost of reduced movement.

<details open markdown="1">
<summary><strong>Suggested implants</strong></summary>

No implant can raise talking above 100% - no social implanting.

</details>

<details open markdown="1">
<summary><strong>Suggested gear</strong></summary>

No specialized gear; they benefit from being near other pawns, so gear them like the majority of your fighting force. Enchanted apparel benefits "trained mages and fighters," and Commander qualifies as a fighter.

**Armor**

- **Disciple Robe** (12) — +20% max energy, +5% energy regen, -5% energy cost, +10% class XP gain, +30% arcane resist.
- **Robes of the Archmage** (25) — +25 max energy, +25% energy regen, -20% ability cooldown, +15% arcane resist.

</details>

### Geomancer

**Good for these jobs:** [Miner](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#miner) (dedicated)

**Bonuses:** +30% Toxic resistance, wider comfortable temperature range (-12°C min / +12°C max), +25% Mining speed, +25% Mining yield, +20 Carrying capacity

**Penalties:** -0.10 Move speed, -5 Melee dodge chance, -20% Global work speed, -15% Mental break threshold, -20% Global learning factor

*Mining Speed and Global Work Speed multiply rather than subtract — the +25%/-20% here nets out to roughly 0% actual speed change. Mining Yield isn't touched by Global Work Speed at all, so that +25% is fully real.*

**Restriction:** None

**Spells learned as part of the class:**
- **Stoneskin** — grants several instances of damage immunity, negating the first few hits taken
- **Loose Stone** — flings stone chunks at a target area
- **Summon Earth Sprite** — turns nearby terrain to rich soil over time, producing magicyte each tile it affects; on infertile ground it just turns the soil over instead
- **Sentinel** — summons a passive guardian that activates on enemy contact; while one's active, Shatter Sentinels detonates all of them at once

**Master spell (needs a scroll):**
- **Meteor** — drops a meteor on the target location, crushing most things it lands on. The resources it contains can then be mined afterward.

<summary><strong>Suggested implants</strong></summary>

Any mining-relevant implant — hands (Manipulation).

</details>

<details open markdown="1">
<summary><strong>Suggested gear</strong></summary>

**Weapons**

- **Staff of Blazing Power** (25) — +20 max energy, +10% energy regen, +40% Damage Boost.

It also drains heat and energy from the wielder and the surrounding area while held — a real cost, not just flavor text.

- **Disciple Hood** (8) — +25% max energy, +5% regen, +15% arcane resist.
- **Arcane Hood** (10) — +10% energy, +10% regen, +10% arcane resist.

- **Mage Robe** (20) — +17.5% energy, -15% ability cooldown.
- **Robes of the Archmage** (25) — +25 energy, -20% ability cooldown.

### Technomancer

**Good for these jobs:** [Researcher](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#researcher) (dedicated), [General Workforce](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#general-workforce) (via Techno-bit), [Pet Class](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#summoner-job) (Techno option)

**Bonuses:** +20% Global work speed, +20% Global learning factor, +25% Research speed, +20% Psychic sensitivity, +5.0 Shooting accuracy

*Research Speed and Global Work Speed both apply to research, and they multiply rather than add — net effective research speed here is closer to +50%, not +25%.*

**Penalties:** -30% Social impact, -20% Negotiation ability, -20% Trade price improvement, -20% Tame animal chance, -20% Train animal chance, -20% Plant work speed, -20% Plant harvest yield, -6% Immunity gain speed

**Restriction:** Technically none, but definitely struggles working with anything biological.

**Spells learned as part of the class:**
- **Techno-bit** — a small drone companion. While undrafted, it helps with mining, crafting, construction, or repair. While drafted, it throws explosives at the Technomancer's target or repairs nearby robotic objects.
- **Mark-IV Turret** — deploys a linked turret, upgradeable with rockets and mortars. Deactivates if the Technomancer moves or does anything else.
- **Engineer Weapon** — copies an existing gun (disappears if separated from the Technomancer, auto-equips), upgradeable for better stats.
- **Overdrive** — pushes powered generators, turrets, or robotic AI past their safety limits for a burst of output, at the risk of stressing or damaging the target system.
- **Sabotage** — the offensive mirror of Overdrive: causes minor to catastrophic failure in nearby powered, computer, or mechanical systems.
- **Kinetic Shield** — deflects projectiles for the target; the shield's power sets the limit of what it can stop, so high-powered rounds can punch through a weak shield.

**Master spell (needs a scroll):**
- **Orbital Strike** — hacks an orbital bombardment satellite to strike the target location. Takes time to align and fire, but hits hard.

### Druid

**Good for these jobs:** [Farmer](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#farmer) (dedicated)

**Bonuses:** +15% Immunity gain speed, +20% Toxic resistance, +25% Plant work speed, +25% Plant harvest yield, +20% Animal gather yield

**Penalties:** -10% Social impact, -10% Negotiation ability, -10% Trade price improvement

**Restriction:** None

**Spells learned as part of the class:**
- **Poison** — manipulates biological matter into a toxin in the target's bloodstream; the more the target moves, the faster it works.
- **Beguile Animal** — either enrages an animal or calms it back down, caster's choice.
- **Regeneration** — heals a target over time. Slower than Paladin's Heal, but can restore even severe injuries given enough time.
- **Cure Disease** — stalls or removes natural diseases. No effect on mechanite diseases.
- **Fertile Lands** — doubles a zone's fertility, so crops grow up to twice as fast depending on the crop.
- **Briar Patch** — grows a ring of thorns that slows and injures anything crossing it.

**Master spell (needs a scroll):**
- **Regrow Limb** — creates a Regrowth seed usable in surgery to regrow a missing limb or organ.

### Fire Mage

**Good for these jobs:** [AoE Blaster](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#aoe-blaster) (dedicated)

**Bonuses:** +0.20 Move speed, +10% Global work speed, +40% Immunity gain speed, +20% Psychic sensitivity, +6% Mental break threshold, +15°C max comfortable temperature, +7°C min comfortable temperature

*That last pair isn't a wider comfort range like Geomancer's — both numbers move up. Fire Mage tolerates heat much better, but actually gets uncomfortable in the cold sooner than an unmodified pawn.*

**Penalties:** -5.0 Shooting accuracy

**Restriction:** None

**Spells learned as part of the class:**
- **Firebolt** — a single bolt of mage fire, likely to set the target alight.
- **Fireclaw** — a wave of fire that expands as it travels.
- **Inner Fire** — a warmth aura on an ally: boosts natural healing and mental break resistance, reduces rest/hunger needs, adds cold resistance. Costs 40 mana to maintain and cuts the caster's own mana regen by 30% while active.
- **Cauterize Wound** — quick healing, but may leave burn scars.
- **Dry Ground** — dries up mud, marsh, and shallow water into usable soil.
- **Ray of Hope** — a small mood and work-productivity buff.

**Master spell (needs a scroll):**
- **Firestorm** — a fiery rain of death and destruction over an area.

### Deathknight

**Good for these jobs:** [Tank](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#tank) (dedicated)

**Bonuses:** +30 Carrying capacity, +30% Pain shock threshold, -25% Psychic sensitivity (a bonus — lower vulnerability to magic and psychic attacks)

**Penalties:** -25% Social impact, -25% Negotiation ability

**Restriction:** None

**Runs on Stamina and a class-specific resource called Hate**, built up through melee combat — not mana.

**Spells learned as part of the class:**
- **Spirit Tap** — absorbs life energy from enemies struck in melee, building rage and restoring Hate and stamina, while healing the Deathknight and nearby allies.
- **Spite** — once enough rage is built, channels it into a spirit attack at medium range. Decent damage, very low cooldown, can stun with upgrades.
- **Grave Blade** — calls spirits of the dead to strike an area, healing the Deathknight for a percentage of damage dealt.
- **Wave of Fear** — amplifies nearby enemies' fear, reducing their resistance to light and dark attacks.

No master spell.

</details>

### Gladiator

**Good for these jobs:** [Tank](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#tank), [Miner](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#miner) (side pick — see the mining speed note below)

**Bonuses:** +0.20 Move speed, +20% Global work speed, +20% Immunity gain speed, -60% Psychic sensitivity, +4.0 Melee dodge chance, +20% Mining speed, +20% Mining yield, +20% Pain shock threshold

*Mining Speed and Global Work Speed both apply here and both bonuses, so they compound rather than just add — actual mining speed is closer to +44%, not +20%. Gladiator's a genuinely strong side-pick for a mining pawn, not just a fighter.*

**Penalties:** -3% Mental break threshold, -30% Global learning factor, -25% Plant work speed, -20% Plant harvest yield, -25% Research speed

**Restriction:** None

**Runs on Stamina, not mana.**

- **Cleave** — a melee strike that also hits nearby enemies; damage scales with weapon quality.
- **Whirlwind** — a flurry of rapid melee attacks.

No master spell confirmed for this class.

### Sniper

**Good for these jobs:** [Hunter](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#hunter) (dedicated)

Tiered class — Sniper is the entry point, Master Sniper and Legendary Sniper are straight upgrades bought on top, not separate choices. Each tier's full stat block:

| Tier | Price | Move speed | Aiming time | Shooting accuracy | Research speed | Global work speed | Tame/Train animal chance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sniper | 30 | +0.15 | -15% | +6.0 | -13% | -13% | -13% |
| Master Sniper | 20 | +0.20 | -22% | +8.0 | -15% | -15% | -15% |
| Legendary Sniper | 20 | +0.25 | -30% | +10.0 | -16% | -16% | -16% |

**Restriction:** Description flavor notes growing social distance from other colonists at each tier — no mechanical restriction, just a roleplay note.

**Runs on Stamina, not mana.**

**Abilities (same across all three tiers — the tiers upgrade the numbers above, not the ability list):**
- **Sniper Training** — a passive that increases ranged combat skill over time.
- **Headshot (Vital Shot)** — targets a weak point for a lethal strike; aims for the head, or another detected weak point if the head isn't available.
- **Disabling Shot** — a non-lethal shot that knocks a target down temporarily, with an option to include a tranquilizer for longer sedation.
- **Anti-Armor Shot** — nanite rounds that destroy armor. Very effective against armored enemies, much less so against anything else.

No separate master spell — the tier system fills that role for this class.

<details open markdown="1">
<summary><strong>Suggested implants</strong></summary>

Eyes (Sight) — Shooting Accuracy is the class's whole identity, and Sight is capped at 200% efficiency for that stat specifically. Archotech eyes (150% Sight each) keep paying off well past 100%, not just a cosmetic upgrade.

</details>

<details open markdown="1">
<summary><strong>Suggested gear</strong></summary>

**Weapons**

Pulled real accuracy and damage stats rather than going by name alone — "sounds like a sniper weapon" isn't always true here.

- **Elephant Bow** (8) — 64% long-range accuracy. Cheapest option, enchanted (magicyte-infused), but the weakest accuracy of the bunch.
- **Bolt-Action Rifle** (8) — 80% long-range accuracy, 5.6 DPS, 1.5s cooldown. Cheaper than the Sniper Rifle and hits harder per shot, at a real accuracy cost.
- **Charge Lance** (12) — 75% long-range accuracy, 6.8 DPS, 2.7s cooldown. More damage than a rifle, slower to fire.
- **Sniper Rifle** (12) — 90% long-range accuracy, 5.0 DPS, 1.5s cooldown. The best long-range accuracy of any weapon in the shop — the name isn't just flavor, the numbers back it up. The correct default pick.
- **Thrum Bow** (20) — 76% long-range accuracy, enchanted. A real alternative if you'd rather go the bow route, priced at a premium for it.

Skip, despite the tempting damage numbers: Charge Rifle/Beam Repeater. High DPS, but very low accuracy.

**Armor**

- **Helm of the Valiant** (12) — -5% energy cost, +10% class XP gain. Pairs with Armor of the Valiant below as a matched set.
- **Paragon's Helm** (12) — -5% energy cost, +15% class XP gain, +5% max energy, +10% arcane resist.

### Necromancer

**Good for these jobs:** [Pet Class](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#summoner-job), [General Workforce](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#general-workforce), [Farmer](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#farmer) (via undead), [Construction](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#construction) (via undead), [Cook](/itemlist/guide/jobs/#cook) (via the Lich evolved form only)

**Bonuses:** -20°C min comfortable temperature, +40% Immunity gain speed, +40% Toxic resistance, +40% Global learning factor

**Penalties:** -0.30 Move speed, -10% Mental break threshold, -50% Trade price improvement, -25% Negotiation ability

**Restriction:** None on the Necromancer directly, but the undead it raises have real limits — see below.

**How undead work:** Raised from dead pawns or animals. They don't eat, sleep, need recreation, or feel pain, and they heal rapidly and resist disease. They're tied to the Necromancer that raised them — separated from every available Necromancer (including by that Necromancer's death), an undead dies within a few in-game hours, unless someone's carrying a Necrotic Orb to stand in. Undead humanlikes can't do intellectual labor and have zero ranged skill, but they can fight in melee and do growing, construction, crafting, and dumb labor. Worth knowing: healing magic damages the undead, so a well-meaning healer in the colony is a real trap for them, not a benefit. Witnessing an undead being raised gives most nearby pawns a -8 mood hit.

You can't install implants in an undead pawn — surgery on them isn't possible. But whatever implants they had installed *before* they died stay on the body and carry over.

Skills work differently than you'd expect: an undead's skill levels are randomized on revival, based on the Necromancer's own skill level at the time of casting — not the corpse's original skills at all. That means a corpse can come back better or worse than it was in life, purely by chance, and killing and re-raising the same corpse re-rolls those skills again. This opens up a real (if twisted) optimization move: install strong implants and hand a living pawn great gear first, then kill and revive them — the implants and gear carry over, and you get to gamble on the skill roll on top of what you already built.

**Spells learned as part of the class:**
- **Raise Undead** — raises a dead pawn or animal. Has its own upgrade tree as it levels: Powered Creation (raises more undead per cast, and stronger ones), Cheating Death (cuts the mana cost to raise and maintain undead), Hastened Undead (faster undead movement and actions).
- **Corpse Explosion** — turns a corpse into a delayed explosive. Its own upgrades: Corpse Mastery (cheaper to cast), Volatile Toxins (shorter delay, bigger radius).
- **Death Mark** — an area curse that debilitates and terrifies nearby pawns.

**Master spell (needs a scroll):**
- **Lich** — transforms the Necromancer into an undead commander:

| Change | Detail |
|---|---|
| Needs | No food, no sleep |
| Resistance | Strong disease immunity, damage resistance, health regen |
| Mana | +50 max, +50% regen |
| Passions | Loses all except Fighting and Research |
| Blocked entirely | Caring, Social, Animals, Firefighting, Artistic, Dumb Labor |
| Still allowed | Construction, Mining, Cooking, Plants, Crafting, Intellectual — at -40% learning speed, -75% global work speed |
| Move speed | -2.0 c/s |
| New spells | Flight (long-range travel, works through walls), Death Bolt (area necrotic-damage attack) |

**Weapons**

Whichever weapon category gets specialized (Pistol, Rifle, or Shotgun) should be the one actually carried — the specialization does nothing for any other category, and a "++" spec weapon in that category is the whole point of the build. Pistol is the pick if "double tap" matters; otherwise base it on whether the fight calls for a rifle's range or a shotgun's close-range burst.

**Armor**

Modern firearms plus vanilla flak/marine armor is the straightforward answer here rather than the usual TMagic robe/plate sets, since none of this class's abilities scale off arcane-flavored gear stats.
